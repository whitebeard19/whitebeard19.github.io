## The problem

Signups were healthy. Activation wasn't. Roughly half of new users never created their first project, and the ones who left all left at the same place: step two, "connect your data".

## The signal

Treat the funnel like a circuit with a leak: probe every node and the voltage drop tells you where.

<Toy title="FUNNEL PROBE">
  <FunnelProbe client:visible />
  <Fragment slot="caption">Share of new signups reaching each step, before the fix. Click a step to probe it. Example data.</Fragment>
</Toy>

Session recordings and five user calls told the same story: people didn't have their credentials at hand, so they left "to get them" and never came back.

## The decision

Three options made it to the whiteboard. Because I'd be the one building it, I could put honest effort estimates next to the product bets.

| Branch | Bet | Effort | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `fix/help-text` | Users are confused | 1 day | closed |
| `feat/sample-data` | Show value before setup | 1.5 wk | **merged** |
| `feat/oauth` | Remove the credentials step | 4 wk | backlog |

<Hat role="SDE HAT">
Sample data meant a demo workspace per user. I kept it cheap with copy-on-write: one shared dataset, cloned only when the user edits it.
</Hat>

## What shipped

New users now land in a working project filled with sample data. Connecting their own data moved to a banner they can act on whenever they're ready. Activation went from [48%] to [71%] in the [four] weeks after launch.

## What I'd change

1. Instrument the funnel *before* the redesign. We lost a week rebuilding baseline numbers.
2. Talk to the users who churned, not only the ones who stayed.
3. Give OAuth a date on the roadmap, so "later" doesn't quietly become "never".
