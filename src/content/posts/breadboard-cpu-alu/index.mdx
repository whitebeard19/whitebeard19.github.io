## What an ALU actually is

The arithmetic logic unit is the part of a CPU that does the maths. In nibble-4 it's one 4-bit adder chip plus a trick: feed B through XOR gates and the same adder can subtract.

## Bill of materials

| Part | What it does | Qty |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| `74HC283` | 4-bit binary full adder | 1 |
| `74HC86` | Quad XOR, flips B when subtracting | 1 |
| 330 Ω resistor | Current limit for each LED | 5 |
| LED, red | Shows result bits + carry (mind the flat side) | 5 |
| Jumper wire | Everything else | ~120 |

<Hat role="SDE HAT">
This XOR trick is two's complement, the same reason `-1` is `0xFFFFFFFF` in your code. Every integer overflow bug you've met was born on a chip like this.
</Hat>
